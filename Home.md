# Macau Events Wiki

A structured, multilingual event intelligence wiki for Macau — covering government calendars, casino resorts, bars, restaurants, nightlife venues, promoters, media, and cultural institutions.

## What this wiki stores

- Current, future, and past events
- Original source URL and source language
- Event images and featured display image
- Ticketing, prices, membership requirements, dress code, age restrictions
- Venue, address, district, transport, parking, accessibility
- Organizer, promoter, DJs, performers, speakers, chefs, curators
- Multilingual wiki information in 12 languages

English, Chinese Cantonese / Traditional Chinese, Chinese Mandarin / Simplified Chinese, Portuguese, Korean, Thai, German, Italian, Spanish, Albanian, Vietnamese, Indonesian.

## Website relationship

The public website should only show current/upcoming events. This wiki remains the complete archive.
